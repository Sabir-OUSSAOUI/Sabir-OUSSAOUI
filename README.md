## From orbit to impact

I use satellite remote sensing, explainable AI and socioeconomic data to monitor and forecast drought in Morocco.

- **Thesis:** *Drought Monitoring and Forecasting in Morocco using Remote Sensing and Socioeconomic Data*
- **Supervisors:** Prof. A. Hadri (UM6P-IWRI), Prof. A. Boudhar (FST-UCA), Prof. C. Kinnard (UQTR-RIVE)
- **Research axes:** agricultural, meteorological and hydrological drought, vegetation phenology, explainable AI, water resources, socioeconomic impacts of drought
- **Teaching:** AI for Water Management (Cadi Ayyad University) and Environmental Geomatics (USMS Beni Mellal)
